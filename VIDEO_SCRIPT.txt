========================================================
ACCSYS FIELD OPERATIONS PLATFORM — INTRO VIDEO SCRIPT
Estimated duration: 8 to 10 minutes
Voice: Male, calm and professional tone
========================================================

[SCENE 1 — INTRO — 0:00 to 0:30]

Welcome to ACCSYS — the Field Operations Platform built for Ratal Group.

This system connects office administration with field teams in real time.
Department Heads, Project Managers, Supervisors, Technicians, Drivers,
and Sub-Contractors all work through one unified platform — no paper,
no WhatsApp chasing, and no manual follow-up.

Let us walk through the complete workflow from start to finish.

---

[SCENE 2 — ADMIN: GENERATE QR TOKEN — 0:30 to 1:30]

Everything starts in the Administration panel.

Before a field employee can use any form, the admin generates a personal
QR token for them.

Go to Administration, then QR Code Manager.

Click "Generate QR Token."

Select the employee from the list — in this case, Ahmed Al-Rashidi,
a Site Technician.

Choose his field role — Staff Technician.

You can set an expiry date if the project is time-limited, or leave it
blank for a permanent token.

Click Generate.

The system instantly creates a unique secure link and displays a QR code.

From here, you can copy the link and share it directly via WhatsApp
or Telegram with one click.

Ahmed receives the link on his phone. That is his personal entry point
to all his field forms.

---

[SCENE 3 — FIELD: LANDING PAGE — 1:30 to 2:15]

When Ahmed opens the link on his phone, the system reads his token,
identifies him, and shows his personal dashboard.

He can see his name, his role, and all the forms he is authorised to use.

No username. No password. The QR token handles authentication securely.

The forms are large, touch-friendly cards — designed entirely for mobile use
in the field, even in bright sunlight.

---

[SCENE 4 — FIELD: DAILY EXPENSE — 2:15 to 3:15]

Ahmed taps "Daily Expense" to record today's field costs.

He selects the date, then picks the expense type — in this case, Fuel.

He enters the amount in Saudi Riyals, adds a brief description,
and attaches a photo of the receipt by tapping the camera icon.

He hits Submit.

The entry is saved instantly to the system. His supervisor and Department
Head can see it immediately in their dashboards.

Over the course of the month, Ahmed logs all his field expenses the same way —
fuel, accommodation, materials, and meals.

---

[SCENE 5 — FIELD: EXPENSE CLAIM — 3:15 to 4:00]

At the end of the month, Ahmed submits his Expense Claim.

He taps "Expense Claim" from his landing page.

The system automatically pulls in all his recorded daily expenses
for the current month, calculates the total, and presents them for review.

Ahmed checks the summary, adds any final notes, and taps Submit Claim.

The claim is now in the system with status "Submitted" and is waiting
for Department Head approval.

---

[SCENE 6 — FIELD: SITE COMPLETION — 4:00 to 4:50]

Ahmed's other key task is updating site progress.

He taps "Site Completion" and selects his assigned site.

The system shows the full milestone checklist for that site's scope —
in this case, Tower Installation.

Ahmed ticks off the milestones completed today — Foundation installed,
Tower section one erected, and Tower section two erected.

He adds a site photo and a brief note, then submits.

The system calculates the updated completion percentage and logs the update
with a timestamp. The Project Manager and Department Head are notified immediately.

---

[SCENE 7 — FIELD: PAYMENT CONFIRMATION — 4:50 to 5:30]

When a Department Head issues a cash advance to a field employee,
the employee receives a notification to confirm receipt.

Ahmed taps "Payment Confirmation."

He can see the advance issued to him — the amount, the date, and who issued it.

He taps to expand the card, uploads a photo of the signed voucher,
adds a note, and confirms receipt.

This creates a complete, auditable trail from cash issuance all the way
to signed confirmation — no paper forms required.

---

[SCENE 8 — FIELD: VEHICLE REPORT — 5:30 to 6:10]

For drivers and supervisors managing fleet vehicles, there is the
Vehicle Report form.

The employee selects the vehicle from the registered fleet,
then chooses the log type — Fuel, Maintenance, Incident, or Inspection.

For a fuel log, he enters the litres filled, the cost, the station name,
and the odometer reading.

For an incident, he uploads up to two photos and describes what happened.
The Department Head receives an immediate notification for any incident
or high-urgency maintenance report.

---

[SCENE 9 — ADMIN: DH COMMAND CENTRE — 6:10 to 7:30]

Now let us switch to the Department Head's view.

The DH opens the Command Centre — a live dashboard with five panels.

The first panel is Cash. It shows every outstanding advance issued to
field employees, colour-coded by age. Green for recent, orange for advances
over thirty days, red for over sixty days. The total outstanding balance
is shown at the top.

The second panel is Sites. All active site assignments are listed with
their current completion percentage as a progress bar.

The third panel is Claims. This is where the DH approves expenses.
Pending claims appear at the top. The DH reviews the claim details
and taps Approve. The employee is notified instantly.

The fourth panel is Team — a full list of all assigned field personnel
with their designation and a direct call button.

The fifth panel is Notifications — all recent alerts from the field,
sorted by newest first. Unread items are highlighted.

---

[SCENE 10 — ADMIN: QR MANAGER & NOTIFICATIONS — 7:30 to 8:15]

Back in the administration panel, the Field Notifications page gives
a system-wide view of every notification sent across all employees and entities —
with Telegram delivery status shown for each one.

If a token needs to be revoked — for example when an employee leaves the project —
the admin goes to QR Manager, finds the token, and clicks the revoke button.
Access is removed instantly. The link no longer works.

New tokens can be generated for new team members in seconds.

---

[SCENE 11 — CLOSING — 8:15 to 8:45]

ACCSYS Field Operations covers the complete cycle:

Cash advances issued and confirmed. Daily expenses recorded and claimed.
Site milestones tracked and approved. Fleet incidents reported in real time.
Sub-contractor work documented and submitted for payment.

All of this from a phone, with no paper, no manual data entry back at the office,
and a full audit trail for every action.

This is just the overview. A detailed training video for each role —
Technician, Supervisor, Department Head, Sub-Contractor, and Driver —
will follow once the Telegram notification system is fully configured.

Thank you for watching.
