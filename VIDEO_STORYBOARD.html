🛠 Production Guide — Free Tools Only

STEP 1 — Generate Voiceover

Open ttsmaker.com (free, no account needed).
Paste VIDEO_SCRIPT.txt content.
Choose voice: English Male → Natural.
Download as MP3.

STEP 2 — Record Screen

Press Win + G → Start Recording.
Follow this storyboard scene by scene.
Play audio through speakers while recording, OR record separately and sync in editing.

STEP 3 — Edit & Combine

Download CapCut Desktop (free).
Import screen recording + MP3 audio.
Drop audio on timeline, trim video clips to match.
Export as MP4.
